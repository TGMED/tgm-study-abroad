    Methodology: exchange rates are live, fetched server-side and cached (see the badge above). The "Monthly salary" you enter is treated as take-home pay (already net of Nigerian tax) — it's deducted only for modeled Lagos/Abuja/Port Harcourt rent (capped at 40% of that take-home, since a real market rent charged flat would crush lower incomes unrealistically), not taxed again. Destination salary, tax and rent figures are a research snapshot (Aug 2026) sourced from official statistics bodies and tax authorities where available — UK: ONS/gov.uk/HMRC; Canada: Job Bank Canada (StatCan), CRA, CMHC; Germany: Entgeltatlas (Bundesagentur für Arbeit), destatis, BMF; Poland: GUS, Ministry of Finance, ZUS; Ireland: CSO, Revenue.ie, RTB. Nigeria rent uses real-estate market reports since no official NBS rent index exists. Study-phase housing costs use shared/dormitory accommodation figures (Studierendenwerk, SpareRoom, Rentals.ca, etc.), not the full post-graduation market rent. A few figures have no government-published occupation-level breakdown and use a named recruitment-market salary survey instead (e.g. Morgan McKinley for several Ireland roles); the returnee-qualification wage premium uses non-Nigeria-specific research (Egypt/West Africa) since no Nigeria-specific study was found. Destination tax calculations use simplified bracket models and omit some credits/allowances. Treat every figure as directional, not exact — this is a snapshot that should be refreshed periodically as tax bands and wages change.
